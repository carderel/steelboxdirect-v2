## IMPORTANT STRUCTURAL NOTE — Virginia independent cities

Virginia uses an independent-city system: the Hampton Roads metro core is made up of
independent CITIES that are not part of any county — Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake,
Portsmouth, Suffolk, Newport News, and Hampton. There is no "county zoning office" for any
of them; zoning is administered by each CITY's own planning/zoning department. The
`counties[]` and `zoning[].county` fields below therefore carry city names (prefixed
"City of …") — this is the honest adaptation, not a forced county model. The nearest true
counties (e.g., Isle of Wight County) were NOT verified this pass and are omitted per RC rules.

Peninsula (Newport News, Hampton) from the Southside means the I-64 Hampton Roads

(Bridge-tunnel facts: VDOT — HRBT on I-64 connects Hampton/Newport News to Norfolk/Virginia

Downtown & Midtown Elizabeth River tunnels are the other two harbor crossings — these are
tolled facilities. Flood/low-elevation character of Norfolk is broadly documented by the
city itself; kept qualitative, no stats.)

## areaProfile (2–3 sentences)

"Hampton Roads is a seven-city coastal metro organized around its harbor: the Port of
Virginia moves containers through Norfolk International Terminals — the port's largest
terminal — and Virginia International Gateway, Naval Station Norfolk is the world's largest
naval station, and Newport News Shipbuilding across the water is Virginia's largest
industrial employer. Away from the waterfront the metro turns genuinely rural — Suffolk and
western Chesapeake are peanut-and-row-crop country, a legacy dating to Planters setting up
in Suffolk in 1912."

- Port claims: portofvirginia.com (VPA operates NIT; NIT = port's largest terminal; VIG leased/operated by port). Grade A.
- "World's largest naval station": cnic.navy.mil (official Navy installation page). Grade A.
- NNS = "largest industrial employer in the Commonwealth of Virginia," ~26,000 shipbuilders: hii.com + vedp.org (VA Economic Development Partnership). Grade A (use the title, not the headcount, in page copy).
- Suffolk peanut heritage + Planters 1912: suffolkva.us/1065/History (official city history page). Grade A.
