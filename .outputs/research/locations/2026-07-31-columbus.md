## zoning[]

**Structural honesty note (this metro genuinely needs it):** in Ohio, zoning is administered by
municipalities and townships, not counties. Inside Columbus city limits the authority is the **City
of Columbus Department of Building & Zoning Services**; in Franklin County's unincorporated area the
**county** zoning resolution covers only ten townships (Brown, Clinton, Franklin, Hamilton, Mifflin,
Madison, Norwich, Pleasant, Sharon, Truro — source 2). So Franklin County gets **two rows** (city +
county) rather than one. The other six rows are the correct county-level starting point a buyer
should call; each of those offices routes to the township zoning inspector where applicable. This is
the same two-row treatment the New York dataset used for citywide zoning.
