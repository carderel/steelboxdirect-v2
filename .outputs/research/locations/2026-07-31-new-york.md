## zoning[]

**Honesty note (structural, not per-borough):** New York City zoning is CITYWIDE — one Zoning Resolution administered by the NYC Department of City Planning, with permits/enforcement through the NYC Department of Buildings. There are NO per-borough zoning authorities, so the five boroughs get two citywide rows rather than five duplicated ones. In Nassau and Westchester, zoning is administered by individual towns, cities, and villages (NY State home rule); the county planning departments are the referral/resource layer and the correct starting point for a buyer.

```
zoning: [
  { county: 'New York City (all five boroughs)', office: 'NYC Department of City Planning — Zoning', url: 'https://www.nyc.gov/content/planning/pages/zoning' },
  { county: 'New York City (all five boroughs)', office: 'NYC Department of Buildings', url: 'https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/index.page' },
```
