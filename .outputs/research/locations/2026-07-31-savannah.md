## areaProfile (2–3 sentences, port claims verified against GPA)

> The Savannah metro is anchored by the Port of Savannah, whose Garden City Terminal the Georgia Ports Authority describes as the largest single container terminal in North America — and the warehousing and distribution belt that has grown up around it in Garden City, Port Wentworth, and Pooler. Beyond the port, the economy runs on aerospace manufacturing (Gulfstream), the military presence at Fort Stewart–Hunter Army Airfield, historic-district tourism, and a fast-industrializing Bryan County corridor around the Hyundai Metaplant, while Effingham and Bulloch counties stay largely agricultural.

Port-claim verification (fetched from gaports.com 2026-07-31, Garden City Terminal page): GPA's own wording — "The single largest and fastest growing container terminal in North America" and "the largest single-operator terminal in the nation at 1,345 acres." "One of the busiest US container ports" is also safe if preferred — Savannah is consistently ranked a top-3/top-4 US container gateway — but the attributed-GPA phrasing above is the most defensible. [Grade A — primary source]
