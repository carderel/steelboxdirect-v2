## The day's one finding: the GSC impression surge is a COVERAGE win, not a ranking win

Owner supplied `user uploads/Search console/Aug/https___steelboxdirect.com_-Performance-on-Search-2026-08-28.xlsx`
and read it as "nearly double" after 08-21. It is more than that, and worth less than that.

**Measured:** 101.0 impressions/day baseline (07-30..08-20, 22 days, 2,222) -> 363.3/day
(08-21..08-26, 6 days, 2,180). Settled 5-day rate 398/day. **3.6x to 3.9x, not 2x.** The ~435/day
plateau held three days, so this is a new level, not a spike. 45% of the month landed in 6 days.

**The catch, stated to the owner first:** clicks did not follow. 15 clicks across the whole 28
days, site CTR ~0.31%. Average position moved the WRONG way, ~44 -> ~47.4. Desktop carries 3,459
impressions at position 51.3; mobile only 923 but at position 26.6. Google is matching far more
queries, on page 5 and deeper.

**Not the cause:** every page shipped in the 08-21..08-26 sprint is absent or negligible in the
report (certification guide, inspector finder, conex-boxes-for-sale, portable-storage guide,
rent-vs-buy calculator, certification blog post). The gain is on pre-existing pages, so the cause
is template-level and site-level, not new content.

**Ranked causes, tier 1:** city-page template expansion (`3a9c97d` delivered prices 08-19,
`5637e81` call CTA 08-19, `669f91b` all-3-sizes Quick Facts 08-24, `15ff7c2` 4th city FAQ 08-24),
and crawl/duplicate unblocking (`91a4b8f` robots + Content Signals 08-19, `78e5f48` trailing-slash
city 301s **on the exact jump date 08-21**, `488abd5` breadcrumb schema fix 08-24). Tier 2:
`907fc0f` GKP vocabulary (08-25, explains the plateau only), product JSON-LD (Product snippets is
a NEW search appearance at 137 impressions / position 76.9), and the owner's 08-21 GBP category
change (the GBP-linked homepage row is 247 impressions at position 4.4 with 4 clicks, the
best-converting row in the whole report).

**Distribution:** location pages hold 2,151 of 4,891 page impressions (44%).
`/locations/indiana/indianapolis-shipping-containers/` alone is 870 (18%) at position 65.8 for
3 clicks.

**Flagged as not-a-win:** ~330 impressions (~11% of listed query impressions) are brand collision
with other "steel box" companies including BigSteelBox Canada and steelbox.fr. Another ~400 are
out-of-area demand SBD cannot serve (New York 195, Houston 129, Kansas City, Charleston, Savannah,
Norfolk, Shreveport, Texas).

**Honest limit stated:** the export's Pages and Queries tabs are 28-day TOTALS with no per-day
split, so attribution is inference from commit timing plus volume distribution, NOT proof.
