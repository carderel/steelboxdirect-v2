## The correction that gates the whole build

**`fromPrice` is not the unit price.** It is `min(pickupUnitPrice, deliveryUnitPrice)`. Found by the centroid
researcher, then orchestrator-verified independently at ZIP 45404:

```
used:20-std   fromPrice $1,440.60 | deliveryUnitPrice $1,492.05 | base $550
              fromPrice + base = $1,990.60     <- what the spec said to build
              delivUnit + base = $2,042.05     <- correct
```

**A $51.45 understatement on Dayton's published price, with every sanity check passing** because both numbers
look plausible. The two fields are identical in Cincinnati, which is why the orchestrator's own $2,040.60
figure held up across two days of analysis and the error went unnoticed until a research agent read the
field semantics properly.

Fixed at the source: four sites patched in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-17-geo-pricing-feed-design.md` (lines 61, 274, 404, 828), rather than passed
verbally to the build agents, so the wrong field cannot leak back in from the document later. The Task 2
agent was additionally told to assert that the delivered total never equals `fromPrice + baseDeliveryCost`
where those differ, which converts a silent understatement into a test failure.

## The other thing the research corrected, and it was the orchestrator's whole argument

The stated rationale for centroid ZIPs was that downtown ZIPs sit nearest the yard and would systematically
understate. **False in this feed.** `baseDeliveryCost` is a flat per-metro zone rate whenever the delivering
yard is local, and only becomes distance-scaled when it is remote. Centroid versus downtown delivered 20ft is
**identical to the cent in 13 of 15 metros**, and in Louisville the centroid is $17.51 CHEAPER, the opposite
of the prediction.

The decision survives on a better reason: the flat zone rate is a current property of the feed, not a
guarantee. If FreedomConex moves to per-mile pricing inside metros, a downtown ZIP silently becomes a best
case and a centroid ZIP does not. Insurance, not arbitrage.
