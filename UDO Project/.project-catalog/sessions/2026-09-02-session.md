## A position I took that the owner corrected, and he was right

I framed social profiles as "distribution, not authority" and stopped there. He pushed back that
others report social posting helps. **He is right and I was too narrow.** Both hold: the links are
nofollow so they pass no PageRank, AND social posts get crawled quickly (surfacing URLs), drive
referral traffic to people who can link, and feed entity recognition, which is what the Maps pack
and AI assistants read. That last mechanism matters most for this project's AEO play. He chose to
drip the posts rather than burst them, which is the better call. He then deferred the drip schedule.
